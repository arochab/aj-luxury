# AJ Luxury accueil — Gauntlet workbench

Status: Round 4 critic FAIL — implementation frozen at the existing-asset ceiling; isolated preview candidate only

Budget: minimum 3 critique rounds; maximum 5; one builder at a time because the homepage units share the same component and stylesheet. Stop only on full PASS, two consecutive rounds without measurable improvement, or round 5.

## Non-negotiable floor

- Existing repository photography only. No generated or recreated asset.
- No video, canvas, `hero-figures`, `hero-v6`, `hero-v7`, poster derived from video, or architectural composite anywhere on the homepage.
- No intro gate, pinned sequence, scrub, autoplay, or motion that delays content.
- Product, headline, and primary action legible in the first viewport at 1440×900 and 390×844.
- No clipped primary content, accidental horizontal overflow, or sub-15px visible copy.
- Keyboard-operable colorways, visible focus, `prefers-reduced-motion`, and WCAG AA contrast.
- Build, retained-asset contract, rendered HTML contract, and targeted lint stay green.

## Bar

Primary reference: Derek Rose homepage, inspected 2026-08-24. AJ must tie or beat it on calm hierarchy, honest product photography, navigation clarity, scroll rhythm, and interaction restraint. Secondary lenses: CDLP for editorial tension and Sunspel for separation between navigation, promise, and commerce; their video heroes are explicitly excluded.

At 1440×900 and 390×844, the first viewport must show the product, `Apollon`, `29,99 €`, and a concrete primary CTA. Product and waistband must occupy at least 25% of the visible viewport. The CTA target is at least 44×44 px. No content panel may exceed 1.5 viewports without introducing new commercial information. Motion is limited to 180–500 ms and one dominant movement per viewport.

Blind A/B protocol: compare AJ and Derek Rose at 0, 0.75, 1.5, 3, and 5 viewport heights on desktop and mobile, with brand/domain labels masked. Grade premium perception, product comprehension, photographic trust, readability, click intent, and narrative flow. A hard PASS requires no non-negotiable failure and no critic preference for the reference on hierarchy, product comprehension, or photographic trust.
